# Combat System

Combat is a core part of the game. Here's what you need to know:

![Fight screen](@guide/fight)

## Basic Combat

When you enter combat, you'll see your creature's stats and available actions. Choose wisely to defeat your opponent.

## Elements

The game features five elements, each with strengths and weaknesses:

- ![Fire](@elements/elem_fire) **Fire** - Strong against Wood, weak against Water
- ![Wood](@elements/elem_wood) **Wood** - Strong against Water, weak against Fire
- ![Water](@elements/elem_water) **Water** - Strong against Fire, weak against Lightning
- ![Lightning](@elements/elem_lightning) **Lightning** - Strong against Water, weak against Air
- ![Air](@elements/elem_air) **Air** - Strong against Lightning, weak against Wood

![Element chart](@guide/elements)

Understanding elemental advantages is crucial for victory.

## Assault Moves

![Assault interface](@guide/assault)

Assault moves are powerful attacks that can turn the tide of battle. Each assault move has:

- A power rating
- An element type
- An energy cost
- Potential status effects

Choose your assaults carefully based on your opponent's weaknesses.

## Status Effects

Various status effects can impact combat:

- ![Sleep](@guide/status_sleep) **Sleep** - Cannot act for several turns
- ![Untouchable](@guide/status_untouchable) **Untouchable** - Immune to damage
- ![Slow](@guide/status_slow_down) **Slow Down** - Reduced speed
- ![Fast](@guide/status_faster) **Faster** - Increased speed
- ![Petrified](@guide/status_petrified) **Petrified** - Cannot act
- ![Assault Bonus](@guide/status_assault_bonus) **Assault Bonus** - Increased attack power
- ![Poisoned](@guide/status_poisoned) **Poisoned** - Takes damage each turn
- ![Locked](@guide/status_locked) **Locked** - Cannot use certain abilities
