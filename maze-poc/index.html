DinoRPG — Maze POC

Fog of war: the maze layout is generated and AES-encrypted on the backend (« pixy ») and never sent to the browser. Every arrow-key step is validated server-side and reveals only the adjacent cells — the maze cannot be solved without exploring it. Rendered with Pixi.js using the original DinoRPG dungeon tileset (gfx/dungeon); the dinoz uses @eternaltwin/dinorpg_animations.
